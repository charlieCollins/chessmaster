# Bishops

*Pieces* · [all topics](README.md)

Long-range diagonal piece: good vs bad bishops, the bishop pair, pins.

## Key ideas

- A bishop is 'bad' when its own pawns are fixed on its colour; put your pawns on the opposite colour to your bishop.
- Two bishops together cover both colours and grow stronger as the position opens.
- Bishops pin and skewer along diagonals — look for a king or queen behind a defended piece.
- With pawns on both sides of the board a bishop usually beats a knight (Capablanca §14, §30).
