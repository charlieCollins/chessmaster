# Pawn endings and the opposition

*Endgames* · [all topics](README.md)

King and pawn endings decide many games: opposition, key squares, outside passed pawns.

## Key ideas

- Get the opposition; when you can't, look for triangulation or a pawn tempo.
- An outside passed pawn decoys the enemy king while yours eats the other side.
- Counting is exact here: calculate the race, don't guess.
