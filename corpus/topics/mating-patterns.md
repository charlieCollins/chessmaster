# Mating patterns

*Combinations* · [all topics](README.md)

Named mates you should recognise instantly — back rank, smothered, Arabian, Anastasia and more.

## Key ideas

- Back-rank mate: a king boxed in by its own pawns; make 'luft' (a flight square) in time.
- Smothered mate: a knight mates a king surrounded by its own pieces, often after a queen sacrifice.
- Learn the pattern, then look for it two or three moves before it appears.
