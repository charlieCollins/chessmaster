# Bishop and knight endings

*Endgames* · [all topics](README.md)

Good bishop vs bad bishop, bishop vs knight, knight endings that behave like pawn endings.

## Key ideas

- Bishop vs knight: open board and pawns on both wings favour the bishop.
- Put your pawns on the opposite colour to your bishop; attack pawns fixed on your bishop's colour.
- Knight endings are like pawn endings — king activity and outside passers decide.
