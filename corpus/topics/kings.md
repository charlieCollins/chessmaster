# Kings

*Pieces* · [all topics](README.md)

Hide it in the middlegame, use it as a fighting piece in the endgame.

## Key ideas

- Castle early and keep the pawns in front of your king unmoved unless there's a reason.
- Once queens are off, centralise the king — it's a strong piece in the ending.
- The opposition: kings facing each other with one square between; the side *not* to move holds it (Capablanca §13).
- In pawn endings the king leads the pawn, not the other way round.
