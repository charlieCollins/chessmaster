# Queens

*Pieces* · [all topics](README.md)

The most powerful piece — and the easiest to chase around if developed too early.

## Key ideas

- Develop minor pieces before the queen; an early queen gets attacked with tempo.
- The queen is the best double-attacker: look for queen forks on loose pieces and the king.
- Queen vs rook is a win but needs technique (Capablanca §16).
- When ahead, offering a queen trade usually helps; when behind, keep queens on for counterplay.
