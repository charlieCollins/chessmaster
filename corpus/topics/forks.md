# Forks and double attacks

*Tactics* · [all topics](README.md)

One move, two threats — the opponent can only answer one.

## Key ideas

- Any piece can fork; knights and queens do it most. Pawn forks are often missed.
- Checks make the best forks: the king must move, the other target falls.
- Loose (undefended) pieces are fork targets — 'loose pieces drop off'.
