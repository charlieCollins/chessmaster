# Pawn structure and weak squares

*Strategy* · [all topics](README.md)

Holes, outposts and pawn majorities — the long-term features plans are built on.

## Key ideas

- A hole is a square no pawn can ever guard again; it's a home for an enemy piece (Capablanca §33).
- A queenside pawn majority makes an outside passed pawn for the endgame.
- Trade pieces toward an ending when your pawn structure is better.
