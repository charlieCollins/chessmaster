# Clearance, interference, in-between moves

*Combinations* · [all topics](README.md)

Move-order tricks: clear a line or square, cut a defender's line, or insert a stronger move first.

## Key ideas

- Clearance: move (often sacrifice) a piece to open a line or square for another.
- Interference: put a piece between a defender and what it defends.
- Intermezzo (zwischenzug): before the 'obvious' recapture, play a forcing move first.
