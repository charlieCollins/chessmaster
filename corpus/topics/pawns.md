# Pawns

*Pieces* · [all topics](README.md)

Pawn structure, passed pawns and promotion — 'the soul of chess' (Philidor).

## Key ideas

- Pawns can't move back: every pawn move leaves squares it can never guard again.
- A passed pawn must be pushed; a protected or outside passed pawn is often decisive in the ending.
- Doubled, isolated and backward pawns are weaknesses mainly when pieces can attack them.
- Count the race: which pawn queens first, and with check? (Capablanca §12).
