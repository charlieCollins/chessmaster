# Discovered attacks and double check

*Tactics* · [all topics](README.md)

Move one piece to unmask another — two attacks for the price of one move.

## Key ideas

- The moving piece can make its own threat (or check) while the piece behind it attacks.
- Discovered check is the strongest version: the opponent must deal with the check first.
- Double check can only be answered by a king move.
