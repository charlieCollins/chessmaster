# Calculation and quiet moves

*Combinations* · [all topics](README.md)

Longer combinations and the quiet move in the middle of them — seeing several moves ahead.

## Key ideas

- Look at forcing moves first: checks, captures, threats — for both sides.
- A quiet move (no check or capture) inside a combination is the hardest move to find.
- Zugzwang: sometimes the strongest move is the one that leaves the opponent without a good move.
