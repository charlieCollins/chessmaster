# Removing the defender

*Combinations* · [all topics](README.md)

Multi-move ideas that win by eliminating, luring away (deflection) or luring in (attraction) a key piece.

## Key ideas

- Find what defends the target, then capture it, deflect it, or overload it with a second job.
- Attraction (decoy): sacrifice to drag a piece — often the king — onto a fatal square.
- These are 2–4 move combinations: calculate the forcing line to the end before you start.
