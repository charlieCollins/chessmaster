# Initiative and planning

*Strategy* · [all topics](README.md)

Keep making threats, attack where you're stronger, and switch sides at the right moment.

## Key ideas

- The initiative: the side making threats dictates play — don't give it away for a pawn.
- Attack on the side where you have more space or more pieces.
- A sudden attack on the other wing works when the defender's pieces are tied up (Capablanca §26).
- A 'safe' passive position can be lost by doing nothing (Capablanca §27).
