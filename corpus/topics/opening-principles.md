# Opening principles

*Strategy* · [all topics](README.md)

Develop, control the centre, castle — and avoid the classic traps.

## Key ideas

- Develop knights and bishops quickly, aiming at the centre; don't move the same piece twice without a reason.
- Control the centre with pawns and pieces (Capablanca §7).
- Castle early; connect the rooks.
- Know the traps in your openings (Capablanca §8).
