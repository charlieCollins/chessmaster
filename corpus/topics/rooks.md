# Rooks

*Pieces* · [all topics](README.md)

Open files, the seventh rank, active rooks in the endgame.

## Key ideas

- Put rooks on open (or half-open) files; doubling them makes the file yours.
- A rook on the seventh rank attacks pawns and pins the king to the back rank.
- In rook endings activity beats material: an active rook is often worth a pawn.
- Rooks belong behind passed pawns — yours or the opponent's.
