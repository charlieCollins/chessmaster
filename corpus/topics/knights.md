# Knights

*Pieces* · [all topics](README.md)

The short-range jumper: forks, outposts, and why a knight needs support points.

## Key ideas

- A knight is best on a protected central square the enemy pawns can't attack (an outpost); on the rim it controls half as many squares.
- Knights are slow: count the moves to get one where it's needed before committing to a plan.
- The knight is the forking piece — always check which two enemy pieces sit a knight's jump apart.
- Knights do well in closed positions with fixed pawns; against a bishop in an open position with pawns on both wings they usually suffer (Capablanca §14).
