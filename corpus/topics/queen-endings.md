# Queen endings

*Endgames* · [all topics](README.md)

Checks everywhere: queen vs rook technique, perpetual check, shepherding a passed pawn.

## Key ideas

- Queen vs rook wins by driving the king to the edge and winning the rook with a fork or zugzwang (Capablanca §16).
- In queen endings, look for perpetual check before anything else when you're worse.
