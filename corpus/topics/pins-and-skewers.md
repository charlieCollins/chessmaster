# Pins and skewers

*Tactics* · [all topics](README.md)

Line attacks: a pinned piece can't (or shouldn't) move; a skewered one must, exposing what's behind.

## Key ideas

- Absolute pin: the piece behind is the king, so the pinned piece can't legally move.
- Attack a pinned piece again — it can't run away.
- A skewer is a pin in reverse: the more valuable piece is in front and must move.
- X-ray: a piece attacks or defends through another piece on the same line.
