# Rook endings

*Endgames* · [all topics](README.md)

The most common endings: active rooks, cutting off the king, rook behind the passed pawn.

## Key ideas

- Activate the rook before grabbing pawns.
- Cut the enemy king off along a rank or file.
- Rooks belong behind passed pawns.
