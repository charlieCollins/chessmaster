# Sacrifices and attacking the king

*Combinations* · [all topics](README.md)

Bring more attackers than defenders, then break the king's shelter — Capablanca's attacking chapters.

## Key ideas

- Attack where you're stronger: count attackers vs defenders around the king.
- The classic Bxh7+ ('Greek gift') needs a knight to follow to g5 and the queen to reach h5 (Capablanca §4).
- A sacrifice is only sound if the follow-up is forcing (checks, captures, threats).
- f7/f2 is the weak point in the opening — defended only by the king.
