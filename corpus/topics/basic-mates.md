# Basic checkmates

*Endgames* · [all topics](README.md)

K+Q, K+R, two bishops, bishop and knight — the techniques every player must know cold.

## Key ideas

- Use your king: the lone king is driven to the edge by your king and piece together.
- K+R vs K: box the king in with the rook and shrink the box (Capablanca diagram 2).
- Bishop and knight mate only in a corner of the bishop's colour (Capablanca §15).
